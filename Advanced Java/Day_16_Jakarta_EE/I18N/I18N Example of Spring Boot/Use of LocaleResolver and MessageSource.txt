LocaleResolver is responsible for determining and setting the current locale based on the incoming request (using headers like Accept-Language, cookies, or session data).
It creates an instance of Locale and passes it to your REST controller method.

In the code, you don't need to directly interact with the LocaleResolver in your controller; it’s handled automatically by Spring. The locale is available for use, and Spring uses it to resolve messages from the MessageSource automatically.


The MessageSource is used to resolve and retrieve messages based on the locale that was set by the LocaleResolver.
