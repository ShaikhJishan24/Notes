Google Disabling Allow Less Secure Apps Option - Alternative Solution

allow less secure apps -ON
	has been disabled from 30th June 2020

You have to generate app password. For that you must follow these steps: 


1) login to your mail account from which you want to send the mail.
2) click on your "profile icon" [ on the right side top]
3) click on "manage your Google Account"
4) new page will appear. in that go to "security"
5) scroll down the screen. you will see an option "2-steps verification" which is by default "off". 
6) in order to Turn it "on", click it.
7) you will get a page "Protect your account with 3 steps verification"
8) click on "Get started"
9) you will be asked to either enter your gmail password (which u use normally) or your phone number.
10) how do you want to get codes generated?
	Text Only
11) wait for some time and check whether you have received text message with the code.
12) enter the code which you have received 
13) you will get a message "it worked, turn on 2-step verification"
14) click on "turn on"
15) the next step is to generate "google app password"
16) click on your "profile icon" [ on the right side top]
click on "manage your Google Account"
17) click on "security"
18) scroll down the screen, you will get an option of "app password"
   click on this option.
19) now you will see a window with following options:
	select app			select device
			generate

20) click on "select app" and type "My Application"
21) click on "generate", you will get "google app password"
22) save this password and use it inside your java application from which you want to send the email.
